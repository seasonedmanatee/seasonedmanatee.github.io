Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Home Mail Monitor is a personal app used only by its owner. It connects the owner's own Gmail account to the owner's self-hosted Home Assistant server.

What data is accessed

The app uses the Gmail scope https://mail.google.com/ to sign in to the owner's mailbox over IMAP. It reads the subject lines and contents of shipping and mail delivery notification emails (for example from USPS, UPS, FedEx and Amazon) received in the last few days.

How data is used

The data is used only to count packages in transit and delivered, and to show USPS Informed Delivery mail images on the owner's home dashboard. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

All processing happens locally on the owner's Home Assistant server. Email data and OAuth tokens are stored only on that server. No data is sold, transferred or shared with any third party, and no external services receive it.

Limited Use

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services, or by removing the integration from Home Assistant.
